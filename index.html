The Maker

Hi, I'm Vaishali.
Welcome to my sanctuary.

Based in Tathawade, Pune, HandKnot Haven is where my problem-solving mindset meets the meditative art of crochet. What started as a hobby to make a flower for my home decor blossomed into a full-time passion.

Every item in this collection is hooked entirely by hand in my studio. I am dedicated to slow fashion—utilizing sustainable yarns, zero-waste practices, and creating pieces with undeniable longevity and soul.

When I'm not crocheting, you can find me sipping chamomile tea, watching vintage movies, or organizing my ever-growing yarn stash!
