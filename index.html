The Magic Behind the Stitches

Hi, I'm Vaishali, the maker behind HandKnot Haven.

What started as a hobby to make a Flower for my home decor blossomed into a full-time passion. There is something profoundly magical about taking a single continuous string of yarn and turning it into something beautiful, functional, and comforting.

Every item in this shop is made entirely by me in my small studio. I focus on slow fashion, sustainable materials, and creating heirlooms that you can pass down or cherish for years.

When I'm not crocheting, you can find me sipping chamomile tea, watching vintage movies, or organizing my ever-growing yarn stash!

Vaishali

Our Premium Materials

The secret to beautiful, long-lasting crochet lies in the quality of the yarn. We source only the best materials for your custom pieces.

Organic Cotton

Perfect for our summer clothing, crop tops, and delicate flowers. Breathable, hypoallergenic, and exceptionally soft against the skin.

Merino Wool

Our go-to for cozy blankets, winter shawls, and thick socks. Temperature-regulating and incredibly warm without the scratchiness.

Premium Blends

Used for durable accessories like phone cases, purses, and charms. These strong, vibrant blends hold their shape and stay bright for years.

Shipping & Delivery

We take pride in safely delivering your handcrafted items. Here is everything you need to know about our shipping process.

Handmade with care. Each piece takes time to craft.

Processing Time

Delivery Time

Returns & Cancellations

Clear and transparent policies because your trust means everything to us.

No Returns

Because every single piece is meticulously handcrafted to order specifically for you, we are unable to accept returns or exchanges.

Damage Replacements

If your handmade item arrives damaged, please reach out to us within 48 hours of delivery with photos, and we will happily provide a replacement.

24-Hour Cancellations

Changed your mind? You can cancel or modify your order within 24 hours of placement. After this window, the crafting process begins!
