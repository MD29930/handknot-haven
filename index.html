Behind the Stitches

Hi, I'm Vaishali.
Welcome to my studio.

What started as a relaxing hobby in my home in Tathawade, Pune, has blossomed into HandKnot Haven. I have a problem-solving mind that loves the strategic challenge of turning a single, continuous string of yarn into functional, beautiful art.

Every single piece in this shop is meticulously hand-hooked by me. I focus on slow fashion, sustainable materials like organic cotton and merino wool, and creating heirloom pieces that carry warmth.
