The Magic Behind the Stitches

Hi, I'm Vaishali.
The maker behind HandKnot Haven.

What started as a hobby to make a Flower for my home decor blossomed into a full-time passion. There is something profoundly magical about taking a single continuous string of yarn and turning it into something beautiful, functional, and comforting.

Every item in this collection is hooked entirely by hand in my studio. I am dedicated to slow fashion—utilizing sustainable yarns, zero-waste practices, and creating pieces with undeniable longevity and soul.

When I'm not crocheting, you can find me sipping chamomile tea, watching vintage movies, or organizing my ever-growing yarn stash!

Custom Made

Have something else in mind?

Custom orders mean absolutely anything you can think of that isn't listed on our store! Crochet is incredibly versatile, allowing us to hook custom color palettes, specific design notes, or unique standalone ideas from scratch.

💡 What counts as a Custom Order? If you saw a design on Pinterest, want a crochet keychain inspired by your favorite hobby, need a customized bouquet made of specific rare flowers, or want a top stitched to your exact measurements, or simply have a unique idea in mind—we can do it! If you can imagine it, we'll work with you to bring it to life.
