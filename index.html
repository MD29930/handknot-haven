The Maker

Hi, I'm Vaishali.
Welcome to my vibrant little studio.

What started as a hobby to make a flower for my own home decor has blossomed into a full-time passion. I have a problem-solving mind that loves the strategic challenge of turning a single continuous string of yarn into something beautiful, functional, and comforting.

Every item in this shop is made entirely by me in my studio. I focus on slow fashion, sustainable materials, and creating pieces that bring an undeniable pop of joy to your life.
